Project 0

Camera, Lens & Perspective
Part 1 — Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way
The reason the close one looks weird has to do with relative distances. When close up the relative distance between your nose and ears for example a lot more i.e the 20 vs 40 matter a lot and leads to the portions of your face closest to the camera looking huge. However when we step back and zoom in now the distortion is less as the relative distances degenerate (200 vs 220) the 20 cm is now small thus letting your facial feature look a lot more natural as the relative distances doesn't cause an exaggeration

Part 2 — Architectural Perspective Compression

Similar principle as in part one but in reverse. Now when we are far away 200 vs 220 again is a very small difference so it looks flatter as the depth matters less. When we come close and zoom out, since we are close the 20m or whatever difference between different parts of the building becomes relatively bigger making it look less compressed as those portions get exaggerated due to the new relation and our closeness.

Part 3 — Dolly Zoom
A combination of both principles. By keeping the subject the same but moving away we notice thing seems more compressed etc. This compression makes it look like the background is getting closer to the subject as the differences between them becomes less and less exaggerated and suddenly the scene looks flatter
